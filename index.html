A friendly “I’d buy that” is not an order.

Most student ventures begin the same way: ask friends, hear “sounds good”, pay for production, and end up with unsold stock.

Demandra replaces guesswork with conditional pre-orders. A founder sets a minimum target and buyers back the idea. If enough people commit, the product is made. If not, nobody loses a rupee, and the founder learns what to change before spending anything.

How it works

From idea to delivered product, with the buyer protected at every step.

1. Founder lists

A product, an early-bird price and a minimum order target.

2. Buyers back it

Validate, say you will buy, or pre-commit with UPI AutoPay. Nothing is debited.

3. Target is checked

At 110%, payments go to escrow. If missed, mandates cancel and nobody pays.

4. Verified delivery

Udyam check, 40% on invoices, 60% after a QR-confirmed pickup.
