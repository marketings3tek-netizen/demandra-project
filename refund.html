Refund policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Demandra is built so most refunds are never needed: you are not charged unless a campaign succeeds.

If a campaign misses its target

Your UPI AutoPay mandate expires automatically. Nothing is debited, so there is nothing to refund.

If a campaign is funded

  • You are charged once, on the campaign end date.
  • The money goes to a nodal escrow account, not to the founder.
  • The founder is paid 40% against vendor invoices and 60% only after delivery is verified by QR scan.

You get a full refund if

  • The founder does not complete MSME (Udyam) registration.
  • The product is not delivered by the promised date, or delivery cannot be verified.
  • The product differs materially from the approved sample or listing.
  • You were charged twice or by mistake.

How to claim

Raise a dispute from your account within 7 days of the promised delivery date. We review within 5 working days. Approved refunds return to the original UPI account within 5 to 7 working days.

Not covered

Change of mind after successful delivery, or damage after you collect the product.
