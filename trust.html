Trust and safety

Trust is the whole product. This is how we protect you.

We never hold your money

Payments run on Razorpay’s RBI-compliant rails into a nodal escrow account.

Staged payouts

Founders get 40% against invoices and 60% only after verified delivery.

Verified ventures

No payout without a valid Udyam MSME certificate.

Pre-screened vendors

Each vendor signs an agreement covering delivery date, quality and sample approval.

Clear refunds

Undelivered or mismatched products are refunded in full.

Your data stays yours

We never store card or UPI PIN details and never sell personal data.

Where your money is, at every stage

StageWhere your money is
Campaign runningIn your bank. Nothing is debited.
Target missedIn your bank. The mandate cancels automatically.
Target met (110%)Collected into a nodal escrow account via Razorpay.
Founder verifiedStill in escrow. 40% is released against vendor invoices.
