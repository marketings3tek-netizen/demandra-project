Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about pre-commits, escrow and refunds.

Will I be charged when I back an idea?+

No. Validate demand and Will buy cost nothing. Pre-commit only approves a UPI AutoPay mandate, and you are debited only if the campaign is funded.

What is the difference between the three options?+

Validate demand and Will buy are free signals that show the founder how much interest exists. Pre-commit is a conditional order and the only one that counts toward the funding target.

What if the founder disappears with the money?+

They cannot. Funds sit in escrow. Only 40% is released against vendor invoices, and 60% is held until you receive the product.

Why 110% and not 100%?+

Some debits fail because of bank downtime or low balance. The buffer keeps a funded campaign funded.

How do I get my product?+

Collect it on campus and scan a QR code. That confirms delivery and releases the final payment.

Can I cancel a pre-commit?+

Yes, any time before the campaign ends. After funding, refunds follow our refund policy.

Is it free for founders?+

Listing is free. We keep 5% of funded campaigns only.

What do brands and incubators get?+

Brands test concepts with real college buyers. Incubators receive registered ventures that already have paying customers.
