How Demandra works

Six steps from idea to delivered product, with your money protected at every one.

1

Create

A founder lists a product, an early-bird price and a minimum order target.

2

Commit

Buyers choose to validate demand, say they will buy, or pre-commit with a UPI AutoPay mandate. Nothing is deducted during the campaign.

3

Validate

If pre-commits reach 110% of the minimum, payments are collected into escrow. The buffer covers failed debits. If the target is missed, mandates expire and nobody pays.

4

Register and release

The founder submits a Udyam MSME certificate. 40% is released against vendor invoices and 60% after QR-verified campus delivery.

5

Incubate

Proven, registered ventures are referred to incubators, EDII-TN cells and seed grant committees.

6

Brand sandbox

FMCG and D2C brands test product concepts with real college buyers.
